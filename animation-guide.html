Recommended Pro Workflow: The AI Agent Shortcut

Copy → Ask Any AI Agent → Generate with Fal.ai H3 Max

Once you click Copy on any hero layout from the vault, head over to your favorite AI agent (such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Antigravity). Simply describe your motion ideas:

"Here is my hero layout. I want to animate this with slow, fluid motion and cinematic studio lighting. Give me an optimized motion prompt for a video generator."

Take that refined prompt and run it through the Fal.ai H3 Max video model (or via the Fal.ai MCP server) to generate a stunning high-definition looping interactive video ready to embed in your hero section.

Implementation Walkthrough: Step-by-Step

How to export from this vault, animate in your chosen engine, and deploy into your frontend stack.

1

Pick & Export your Static Anchor Image

Select any of the 65 layouts from the directory. Download the artwork as a high-resolution PNG or WebP. This serves as the first-frame initialization anchor.

2

Generate Motion via Fal.ai H3 Max or Higgsfield AI

Feed the image into Fal.ai H3 Max with a camera motion prompt: "Slow continuous subtle fluid mercury ripples, cinematic studio lighting, seamless loop" or use Higgsfield for orbital 3D camera pan.

3

Embed with Video or WebGL Canvas & Fallbacks

Optimize the resulting video as an autoplaying, muted, looped WebM/H.264 mp4. Layer your typographic elements, buttons, and badges with CSS mix-blend-mode or backdrop-filter for native glassmorphism.
